# Rob Bridges portfolio

The words used for Rob's works and how the site presents them. Use these names in code, comments, and `references/links.md`.

## Language

**Release**:
One album, EP, or single on the Music page, under one heading in `references/links.md`.
_Avoid_: Album (when a single is meant too), record

**Track**:
One song inside a Release.
_Avoid_: Song

**Player**:
Bandcamp's own embedded player for one Release or one Track.
_Avoid_: Widget, embed

**Gone release**:
A Release or Track that Bandcamp has taken down. The site shows a gray panel in place of its Player.
_Avoid_: Removed, dead, broken player

**Collaboration**:
A work Rob made with another artist, listed on the Collaborations page, with a Player when it has one.
_Avoid_: Feature, collab (in prose)
